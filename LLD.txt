In system design, the focus is on architecture. 
In LLD, the focus is on implementation.
  the discussion is about methods, relationships, data models and state transitions.
  we are expected to write class definitions, method signatures and bit of a logic.
  design patterns are important - you should be able to tell why strategy design pattern is used vs factory design pattern.
  Establish the scope before you start designing.

------
Framework: 

1. Requirements
  Primary capabilities: what operation must this system support?
  Rules and Completion: what conditions define success, failure, or when the system stops or transitions state? 
  Error handling: how should system respond when the inputs or actions are invalid? 
  Scope boundaries: what areas are in scope and what areas are explictly out?

2. Entities and Relationships
  Identify the entities
  Then, think through how they interact to get relationships.

3. Class Design
  Go through entity-by-entity, top-down (Eg: In Tic Tac Toe game, 
Start with the Orchestrator - Game, then the supporting entities like Board, Player etc.)
