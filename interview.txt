We follow cloud native design, where the business functionality has segregated into small, independent Microservices.


What we have is cloud-native in the sense that we use microservices and async processing, and we separate reads into Redis. But we still share a single BRM system-of-record database across services, which introduces coupling. So architecturally it’s closer to CQRS with a shared database, and it has distributed-monolith risks. A more mature cloud-native microservice approach would move toward service-owned data stores and event-driven integration
